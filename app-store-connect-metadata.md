## Description
SurvivalCraft Pro is a short preparedness scenario. Players walk The Dry Mile — water, shelter, pack priority, and a care decision — and keep a streak. It is a training game, not a certification and not live intel.

What’s included:
- One playable four-leg scenario with persisted progress
- Field cards (reading caps below a completed drill)
- Daily drills that clear from the scenario
- Static demo wire copy
- Field notes. No camera identification.

Safety:
- Educational app.
- Restricted modules are non-actionable by design.
- No step-by-step instructions for harm, wrongdoing, or illegal activity.

## Demo tiers (not purchases)
- Free: The Dry Mile, basic field cards, notes
- Pro: Pro field cards and the medical inventory check. Not a certification.
- Elite: age-checked educational pages. Not a certification.
